# Best option for the audience: the thresholds

When a kids or family night leans away and nothing in its audience leans in, Homestand offers the strongest same-audience theme, the way a promotions director would ("run the kids theme your town likes most").

- It must be clearly better than the planned night: a gain of at least 1.0 on the local pull (a full band, since the "Leans in" and "Leans away" cut-offs are 1.0 apart).
- It must not itself lean away: about this town's usual at worst.
- It is labelled "Best kids option here (about this town's usual)", and its reason says it is the strongest choice, not that it leans in.
- A well-known discovered title (popularity 0.97 or more, a big theatrical name, at least typical here) can fill the same role.

The constant is `BEST_OPTION_GAIN` in `src/app/propose/propose.ts`.
